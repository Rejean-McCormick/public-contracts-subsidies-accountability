# Accounting and Accountability Glossary

**Authorization:** Permission to commit funds, not necessarily payment.

**Contract ceiling:** Maximum allowed contract value, not actual cash spent.

**Disbursement:** Cash actually paid.

**Amendment:** Formal change to a contract's price, scope or term.

**Efficient-cost counterfactual:** Defensible cost range for an equivalent outcome.

**Lifecycle cost:** Acquisition, operation, maintenance, upgrades and disposal.

**Grant:** Transfer of support generally not structured as a repayable asset.

**Loan:** Debt with repayment obligations and possibly security.

**Guarantee:** Contingent liability triggered by specified conditions.

**Debenture:** Debt instrument with contractual repayment and possibly conversion terms.

**Equity:** Ownership interest whose future value may rise or fall.

**Impairment:** Accounting reduction of an asset's carrying value.

**Recovery:** Money or asset value actually obtained through repayment, settlement or sale.

**Gross margin:** Revenue less specified direct costs; not net profit.

**Net profit:** Earnings after applicable expenses.

**Opportunity cost:** Value of a feasible alternative not selected.

**Conflict of interest:** Situation where private interests may affect official duties, assessed under applicable rules.

**Right of reply:** Opportunity for affected parties to respond to material criticism.

**Evidence grade:** Classification of documentary support, not a moral or legal judgment.
