# Contributing

Contributions should identify a precise claim, legal entity, accounting period, original source URL and page. Specify whether money was authorized, contracted, paid, impaired or recovered.

Do not treat supplier margins as net profits or allegations as court findings. Publish alternative explanations and uncertainty. Provide a fair opportunity to respond to material criticism.

For new cases, use docs/CASE_TEMPLATE.md. For corrected numbers, record the previous value, new value, source and reason. Update the source register and case index where necessary.
