# FAQ

**Does this cover all government spending?** No. It investigates specific major contracts, corporate support and governance decisions.

**Does an overrun prove waste?** No. The initial price, changed scope, asset value and efficient alternative all matter.

**Is a subsidy automatically a loss?** No. Loans, equity, grants and guarantees have different rights and recovery prospects.

**Are intermediary margins illegal?** Not inherently. Legitimate services and costs must be examined.

**Are all cases proven scandals?** No. Evidence grade C explicitly denotes a research lead.

**Has the repository been updated on GitHub?** No. This archive must be imported or pushed separately.
