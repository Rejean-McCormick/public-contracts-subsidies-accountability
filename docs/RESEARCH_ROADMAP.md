# Research Roadmap

**First:** Reconstruct SAAQclic's initial price and amendments, ArriveCAN's supplier chain, Northvolt's secured loan and debenture, and federal consulting deliverables.

**Second:** Build multi-year executive compensation and business expense tables for Hydro-Québec, CDPQ and other public organizations, while preserving Centraide's functional accounting distinctions.

**Third:** Create instrument-level industrial financing ledgers for Nemaska, Lion Electric, Bombardier, Medicago and other companies.

**Fourth:** Document judgments, settlements, actual recoveries and recommendation implementation for historical inquiries and infrastructure projects.

A mature case needs a chronology, initial-price counterfactual, reconciled ledger, beneficiary map, governance record, recovery assessment, primary sources and corrections.
