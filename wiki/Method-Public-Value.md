# Public Value and Opportunity Cost

## Core method
Evaluate useful services, employment, strategic capacity, intellectual property, emissions benefits and infrastructure against feasible alternatives known at the decision date. Avoid hindsight bias and unsupported monetary valuations.

## Reproducible work product
Identify the institution, legal entity, time period, initial baseline, revised scope, source document, currency, uncertainty and evidence still needed. Record assumptions and preserve dated estimates. Prefer tables that reconcile individual transactions to official totals.

## Application to case studies
Use the method to test value for money, governance and public risk without assuming improper conduct. Compare equivalent alternatives, document contradictory evidence and distinguish contractual responsibility from criminal liability.

## Questions for further investigation
1. What was known at the decision date?
2. What original records establish the relevant amounts?
3. How could the working conclusion be disproved?
4. Which data are missing?
5. What corrections would new evidence require?
