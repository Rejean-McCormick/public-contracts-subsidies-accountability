# Editorial Policy

This project focuses on major procurement, corporate financing, intermediaries, executive accountability and recoveries, not overall public spending.

Prefer signed contracts, audited financial statements, official audits, judgments, public accounts and regulator filings. Cite document dates and pages. A source can establish a number without establishing motive or legal culpability.

Distinguish facts, estimates, interpretations, hypotheses and allegations. Consider legitimate changes in scope, emergency conditions, public benefits and residual assets. Protect irrelevant private information.

Record corrections transparently and publish substantive responses fairly.
