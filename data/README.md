# Research Data

- cases.csv: inventory and evidence grades.
- sources.csv: primary document URLs.
- verification_backlog.csv: missing records.
- financial_observations.csv: selected sourced observations.
- contracts_template.csv: contract ledger schema.
- instruments_template.csv: grants, loans, equity and guarantees.
- executive_cost_template.csv: compensation and separate expense categories.
- decision_log_template.csv: governance chronology.

**Never sum financial_observations.csv as one number.** It mixes different units, accounting classifications, nested components, dates and entities.
