# Cross-Case Analysis

## Original price versus overrun
A project can remain within budget yet have an inefficient starting price. Conversely, a justified scope expansion can produce a higher final price. Both questions require equivalent-cost benchmarks.

## Intermediary economics
Prime suppliers and staffing firms can add real services, but opaque chains can make it difficult to assess price and performance. The spread between invoice and worker compensation is not automatically profit.

## Public downside and private upside
Grants, loans, equity, guarantees and land transactions distribute risks differently. Evaluate the public's legal rights, expected benefits and actual recoveries.

## Leadership and incentives
Analyze total compensation, performance periods, board approvals and separate business expenses. High remuneration alone does not prove misconduct.

## Permanent public capability
Repeated reliance on consultants may reduce institutional knowledge. Compare lifecycle costs and the value of retaining technical, procurement and financial expertise.

## Accountability
Audit findings and inquiry reports should be followed by documented implementation, enforcement and recovery. Do not confuse allegations, administrative criticism and court judgments.

**These are hypotheses and mechanisms for investigation, not findings that every listed institution or contractor acted improperly.**
